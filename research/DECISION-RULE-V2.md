# DECISION RULE v2 — corrected
**This replaces the "four archetypes" framing in `STRATEGY.md` and on the first draft-day sheet.**

## What was wrong
I wrote the rule as slot-filling: *"I am the closer/presenter. I need (1) someone who makes it look good, (2) someone who owns the deadline, (3) someone who can build, (4) someone who gets things for free."*

Two problems, both Ryan's catch:

1. **It can hand a pick to a competent-but-unremarkable person because they fill a box.** "Owns the deadline" is not a reason to draft anyone. Plenty of organised people are not going to win you a weekly challenge.
2. **It treats presenting as Ryan's seat.** Len's own memo: *"More than 70% of your Team's grade will result from successful Rocket Pitches and Skits."* Every teammate will stand up and pitch. Presentation is a **screen applied to every single pick**, not one person's job.

## The rule
> **TAKE THE BEST PLAYER AVAILABLE.**
> **Proven follow-through first. Raw strength second. Can they carry a pitch — asked of everyone.**
> Complementarity is a **tiebreaker between two close players**, never a reason to take the weaker one.
> The only hard constraint: don't end up with four of the same person.

## Re-weighted rubric
| Dimension | Was | **Now** | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proven drive & follow-through | 40% | **45%** | The thing Ryan actually wants: does it come back done, without chasing |
| Raw strength — **including presenting** | 25% | **30%** | Presenting folded in here and applied to everyone, because it is 70% of the grade |
| Diversification | 15% | **15%** | Unchanged — Len scores it explicitly |
| Complementarity to Ryan | 20% | **10%** | Demoted to a tiebreaker |

## What "GOAT" looks like in the evidence — the screen I actually applied
A player scores high on drive only if at least two of these are true and **verifiable**:
- Held one thing for **2+ years** (not a stack of ten-week summers)
- **Shipped something that exists** — a product, a registered entity, a delivered result, a published artifact
- Did work **nobody assigned them**
- **Quantified outcome in their own scope**, not their employer's
- Did the **unglamorous** version — service work, logistics, packing boxes, cold outreach
- **Performs under judgment** — competition, stage, a room of strangers
